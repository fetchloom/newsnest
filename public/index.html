Questions

Straight answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. No plans, no limits we set, no cut of what you earn. You pay only for where you run it (a small server costs a few dollars a month, or use your own computer) and for a domain name if you want one.

Do I need to be technical?

You should be comfortable following a step-by-step guide. If you can set up a website, you can set this up. A hosted version for people who would rather not is coming; leave your email above and we will tell you.

Will my emails reach people's inboxes?

That depends on how you send, not on Newsnest itself. The easiest route is to connect an email service you already use, and Newsnest walks you through the few settings that keep your emails out of spam.

How is this different from Beehiiv or Substack?

You run it yourself, so your list and your data stay with you, and nobody can change the price or close your account. Writing, sending, growing, automations and analytics are all here. What you will not find is a built-in ad network, newsletter recommendations or paid subscriptions.
