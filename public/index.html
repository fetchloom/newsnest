How it works

          
The catch, up front.

          
Free means we charge nothing. It does not mean mail delivers itself. Inbox delivery needs a server with a clean IP address and an open port 25, or an SMTP relay you already have.

        


        
    
          
  1. 01
    Run it
    Clone the repo and start one process. There are no dependencies to install.
    $ node server.ts
    2. 
          
  2. 02
    Point your domain
    Add the SPF and DKIM records Freepost gives you. It re-checks until they pass.
    TXT _dkim.yourdomain.com
    3. 
          
  3. 03
    Choose how to send
    Deliver directly from your own server, or hand off to any SMTP relay you already have.
    mode direct | relay | outbox

FAQ

          
Straight answers.

        

        

          
Is it really free?
Yes. There are no plans, usage limits or revenue share. You pay for whatever you run it on, and for a domain if you want one.

          
Will my emails reach the inbox?
That depends on your sending setup, not on the software. Send from a server with a clean IP address and open port 25, or hand mail to an SMTP relay you already use. Either way you add SPF and DKIM records, and Freepost checks them for you.

          
What's different from Resend and Beehiiv?
Freepost runs on your machine, so there is no hosted network around it. That means no ad network, no recommendations between newsletters, and no built-in paid subscriptions. Writing, sending, segmenting, automating and analytics are all here.

          
Where does my data live?
In one SQLite file on your machine. Back it up by copying it.

          
What do I need to run it?
Node 24 or newer. There is nothing to install: no packages, no build step, no separate database.
